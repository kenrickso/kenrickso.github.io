# Aspirant — LaTeX playground ✨

Type inline math like $e^{i\pi}+1=0$ or display math:

$$
\int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{-x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

You can also write multi-line aligned equations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
a^2 + b^2 &= c^2 \\
\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2} &= \frac{\pi^2}{6}
\end{aligned}
$$

> Tip: Use `$...$` for inline and `$$...$$` for display math. KaTeX renders automatically.
